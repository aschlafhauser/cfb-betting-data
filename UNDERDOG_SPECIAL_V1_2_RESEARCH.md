# Underdog Special v1.2 — Prospective Upset Viability Research Layer

Effective prospectively beginning September 3, 2026. This file does not retroactively alter Underdog Special v1.0/v1.1 history and does not create an official wager by itself.

## Purpose

Week 1's UMass–Rutgers game demonstrated why moneyline-upset research should consider the *shape and variance of the matchup* in addition to spread-model disagreement. The Longshot Upset Lab is intended to identify underdogs whose path to winning outright may be materially wider than a simple spread-to-moneyline conversion suggests.

## Two strategy bands

### Core Underdog Special

Retain the existing v1.1 +3.5-to-+7 framework and its frozen prospective activation requirements unless separately versioned.

### Longshot Upset Lab

Screen all FBS-vs-FBS underdogs priced at **+300 or longer**. These are research candidates, not automatic wagers. Price bands should be tracked separately:

- +300 to +499 — playable longshot band
- +500 to +999 — high-variance band
- +1000 to +1999 — extreme longshot band
- +2000 or longer — lottery-tail band

Do not qualify a candidate merely because the price is large.

## Upset Viability Score (0–100)

Use six matchup dimensions:

1. **Football / Price Edge — 30%**
   - Independent football probability versus implied moneyline probability when genuinely available.
   - If no clean independent probability exists, do not fabricate one; use available final model context and label the component lower-confidence.

2. **Quarterback Upside — 20%**
   - Efficiency, mobility, explosiveness, experience, sack/turnover avoidance, and ability to create offense outside structure.

3. **Game-Compression Ability — 15%**
   - Credible rushing game, clock control, low negative-play rate, manageable down-and-distance profile, and/or tempo control that can reduce possessions.

4. **Favorite Volatility — 15%**
   - New QB, coach/coordinator transition, large roster turnover, OL uncertainty, major injury/suspension risk, or other reasons the favorite's true Week 1 distribution may be wider than its power rating.

5. **Explosive / Turnover Path — 10%**
   - Underdog ability to generate explosive plays, pressure, takeaways, special-teams leverage, and short fields. Do not forecast specific turnover counts; score the credible mechanism for creating variance.

6. **Brand / Market Inflation — 10%**
   - Potential overpricing from conference logo, ranking, home-field reputation, prior-season record, or public brand when underlying roster/matchup evidence is weaker than the headline gap suggests.

## Research classifications

- **75+ — Priority Deep Dive:** complete full matchup research and verify best live ML.
- **65–74 — Live Longshot:** keep on active price/injury watch; may qualify only with positive price edge and no major unresolved risk.
- **55–64 — Watch:** interesting upset path but insufficient evidence/value for activation.
- **Below 55 — Pass:** large odds alone are not a thesis.

For +1000 or longer candidates, require at least a **75 Upset Viability Score** plus a clearly documented quarterback/offensive path and favorite-volatility case before considering even a very small prospective position.

## UMass–Rutgers learning marker

The September 3, 2026 UMass–Rutgers game is a research observation, not a retroactively activated bet. The upset profile visible in-game included quarterback efficiency/mobility, zero offensive turnovers at the observation point, sustained yards-per-play competence, a viable rushing/possession approach, and substantial favorite transition/turnover volatility. Preserve it as a learning example; do not score the pregame candidate using hindsight-only facts.

## Required longshot candidate record

For each Priority Deep Dive / Live Longshot preserve:

- season/week/game
- verified spread and moneyline, source, timestamp
- implied ML probability
- model/fair probability if genuinely available
- Upset Viability Score and six component scores
- QB thesis
- compression/rushing thesis
- favorite-volatility flags
- explosive/turnover path
- possible brand/market inflation
- information-quality grade and unresolved risks
- expert-intelligence residual, including contradictions
- price target / maximum acceptable ML
- classification: PRIORITY DEEP DIVE, LIVE LONGSHOT, WATCH, PASS, or OFFICIAL LONGSHOT if a future explicitly versioned activation rule qualifies

## Discipline

- The Longshot Upset Lab is prospective research first. It does not manufacture official bets.
- Do not backfill candidates after knowing outcomes.
- Track closing line/value and outright results for every recorded Priority Deep Dive and Live Longshot so thresholds can be calibrated after sufficient sample size.
- Avoid treating correlated experts as independent confirmation.
- A recognizable underdog or rivalry narrative is not sufficient by itself.
- Large moneyline payouts do not compensate for a probability estimate that remains below the market-implied break-even probability.
